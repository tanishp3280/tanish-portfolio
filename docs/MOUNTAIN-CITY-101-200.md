Some clouds should drift around cliffs, others should curl around towers, and certain areas should contain denser clouds that part as the camera approaches. The underlying implementation can use a limited set of actual lights combined with emissive materials and carefully managed bloom, rather than rendering hundreds of expensive dynamic light sources.

Introduce subtle physical reactions into the buildings themselves. Wooden planks can flex very slightly as characters walk across them, rope bridges can sway under movement, and hanging signs can bounce after being touched. Materials should have distinct movement characteristics: paper is light, cloth is flexible, wood is springy, and stone is heavy. This creates a consistent physical language across the whole world.
